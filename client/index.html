The background is a live simulation of a complex scalar field in the Higgs potential V(φ) = −μ²|φ|² + λ|φ|⁴. The origin is unstable, so the minima form a circle at |φ| = v — the ring you can see. The field picks one point on that ring and breaks the symmetry; the drifting highlight is the Goldstone mode running around it. The particles crossing the screen each couple to the condensate with a different strength g, pick up mass m = g·v, and the heavy ones visibly slow down and drag a wake. Nothing is pre-rendered, and it stops when the tab is hidden or when you ask for reduced motion.
