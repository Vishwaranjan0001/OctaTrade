001 / Wallet

Virtual rupees, real prices.

Every new wallet starts with ₹1,00,000 of practice money, and you can add more. Balances are kept in paise, so they never drift by a fraction of a rupee.

002 / Engine

Open the engine up.

Your order does not run inside the web request. It waits in a Redis queue, and three worker processes pick it up, the way a broker's back office does.

003 / Scheduler

Fair to every trader.

The scheduler mixes priority, first come first served and round robin, so one busy user cannot starve everyone else. Waiting orders age upward.

Questions

Straight answers.

Is any real money involved?

No. Your wallet holds virtual rupees only. You cannot deposit or withdraw real money, and nothing you do here places a real trade.

Where do the prices come from?

From Yahoo Finance. Quotes are cached for up to 5 seconds and can be delayed, so treat them as close to live, not exchange-grade.

Which order types can I place?

Market orders, buy or sell, in whole shares. Limit and stop orders are not built yet.

Why does my order say PENDING?

Orders wait in a queue until a worker picks them up. If the worker pool is not running, the order stays pending until it starts.
