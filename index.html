Questions

Straight answers.

Is any real money involved?

No. Your wallet holds virtual rupees only. You cannot deposit or withdraw real money, and nothing you do here places a real trade.

Where do the prices come from?

Market data is provided by Yahoo Finance and may be delayed or unavailable. Simulated fills can differ from real trading, where liquidity, slippage and other costs affect execution.

Which order types can I place?

Market orders, buy or sell, in whole shares. Limit and stop orders are not built yet.

How much virtual money do I start with?

Your account starts with ₹1,00,000 in virtual funds. You can add more from your wallet to continue practising. Virtual funds have no cash value.

Can I follow stocks before trading?

Yes. Add NSE stocks to your watchlist, check their latest available quotes and explore their charts before making a practice trade.

How can I review my performance?

Your portfolio shows holdings and unrealised profit or loss. Analytics adds allocation, cash balance and a comparison with the Nifty 50. Your trade history records your simulated buys and sells.
